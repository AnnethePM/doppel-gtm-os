# Doppel Brand Voice

Source: `context/docs/research/voice.md` (checked 2026-11-12). Phrases are exact from Doppel's own pages. The tone notes are INFERRED from those phrases.

## Tone of voice (INFERRED)

| Trait | What it looks like | Example (exact) |
|---|---|---|
| Confident, category-defining | Claims the category outright | "The world's first platform for Social Engineering Defense." |
| Short contrasts | Two short sentences: them, then us | "Modern threats operate at machine speed. Traditional defenses don't." |
| Active, offensive verbs | Takes down rather than monitors | "Dismantle threats and protect your brand's reputation" |
| Playful toward incumbents | Light humor aimed at the old way | "KnowBe4 was built for yesterday's attacks." |
| Proof by speed and volume | Results are takedown times and counts | "Results That Speak for Themselves" |
