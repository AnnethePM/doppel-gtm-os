# Doppel Messaging

Source: `context/docs/research/` only (checked 2026-11-12). The core message and pillars are INFERRED groupings of sourced claims.

## Core message

Attackers now impersonate brands and executives across every channel at machine speed. Doppel finds these attacks as connected campaigns and takes them down at the source, in days instead of weeks, without manual reporting.

Built from: "Modern threats operate at machine speed. Traditional defenses don't." (`voice.md`); "Stop Malicious Campaigns, Not Just Messages" (`voice.md`); Advance's domain takedowns going from 30 days to under a week (`customers.md`).

## Message pillars

| Pillar | Claim | Proof points |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Takedowns at speed and volume** | Impersonations come down in days, not weeks or months | Advance: "75%+" faster takedowns, "25x" more takedowns. ARK: "98%" success rate, "5.5-Hours" median domain resolution. Coinbase: "over 800 social media accounts and 1000+ fraudulent domains" (`customers.md`) |
| **2. Every channel, one view** | One platform sees the whole campaign across web, social, messaging, ads, app stores, email and the dark web | Channels: `company.md`. UniSat: 216 of 289 social detections on X. Unnamed financial services firm: ads using its branding found on Facebook and Google. Sugar23: takedowns across Instagram, Twitter, Facebook and TikTok (`customers.md`) |
| **3. Less manual work** | Onboarding in minutes and automated takedowns replace manual reports and cease-and-desist letters | Advance: "It was a single email." REA: "has removed a huge amount of manual work for us". Dapper Labs: "on autopilot" (`customers.md`) |
| **4. Protect your people, not just your brand** | Executive protection plus deepfake simulation and training | "a 93% reduction in exposed executive PII" (Fortune 500 CISO, `customers.md`). Simulation: "9 min" average conversation with a deepfake, and "100%" "believed the AI was human" (sample size not found, `company.md`) |
