## Case studies

### Advance (financial services, North America) [P]
https://www.doppel.com/customers/advance (re-read 2026-11-12)
- Description: "Advance is a family-owned holding company and private equity firm whose portfolio includes some of the world's most iconic media, entertainment, technology, lifestyle, and news brands." Size: not stated.
- Products used: Brand Protection and Executive Protection, plus Phishing Triage for emails reported by users.
- Buyer and user: Christopher Smedberg, Director of Cybersecurity. He secures the corporate enterprise and helps portfolio companies mature their defenses.
- Before Doppel: "Simple domain takedowns would drag on for 30 days or fail entirely." Its tools were "legacy attack surface management and generic threat intelligence tools." The workflow was "fragmented", with "highly varied and inaccurate data sets". Reports of fake accounts to Meta "resulted in little to no action." Teams relied on "manual legal cease-and-desist processes." Previous vendor: not named.
- Evaluation method (POC or comparison): not described.
- Onboarding: done "in minutes" through "lightweight API integrations" and "a single baseline asset ledger". Smedberg: "It was a single email."
- Channels: domains, social media accounts, fake corporate profiles, executive impersonation, and email reported by users. Takedowns happen at "the registrar and social media platform layers."
- More quotes from Smedberg:
  - "We feel really good about having the agentic automation narrow that funnel for us."
  - "When the teams internally see the success of a takedown, it motivates them to prioritize it more and do that work faster."
- Results: "Minutes" to deploy, "75%+" faster takedowns and a "25x" increase in takedowns. Takedowns went from about 10 a year to over 150 in year one and 250 in year two. Domain takedown time fell from an average of 30 days to under a week.
- Quotes, all from Christopher Smedberg, Director of Cybersecurity:
  - "The best thing about the experience with Doppel is the speed."
  - "What stood out to me the most about Doppel has been the partnership we've developed,"
  - "Attackers are looking for that easy backdoor,"

## Patterns (INFERRED)
- INFERRED: The buyers quoted are mostly security leaders (CISO, Head of Cyber Defence, Director of Cybersecurity). Others are legal (Deputy GC, GC), brand or marketing (Brand Marketing Manager), and Trust & Safety.
- INFERRED: The most common story is replacing an incumbent brand protection or DRP vendor. Speed and volume of takedowns, plus less manual work, are the main proof points. No case study names the previous vendor.
- INFERRED: Crypto and web3 dominate the older stories (2023 to 2024). Financial services, law firms, and Fortune 500 companies dominate the newer ones. That fits the move upmarket described in company.md.
- Human Risk Management and Email Security case studies: **Not found.** Every case study read is about brand protection or executive protection.
