# Doppel: Competitors

Raw research, not conclusions. All sources checked 2026-11-12.
Source quality key: **[P]** = vendor's own page, read directly. **[3P-read]** = third-party page, read directly. **[3P-search]** = seen only as a search snippet.

Doppel sells into three budgets (see company.md): Digital Risk Protection (DRP) and brand protection, Human Risk Management (HRM) and security awareness, and Email Security. Competitors are grouped by those budgets below. The grouping is INFERRED from Doppel's three pillars.

## Observations (INFERRED)
- INFERRED: Doppel is the only vendor here that spans DRP, HRM and Email Security under one "social engineering defense" category name. KnowBe4 and Adaptive span HRM and email, but they have no external takedown product. BrandShield, ZeroFox, Netcraft and Allure have no HRM product.
- INFERRED: In DRP, every rival now claims "AI" and speed. On published takedown times, Doppel is not the fastest: Netcraft claims "33 min." (unconfirmed) and Allure "~15 min" to block, while Doppel's homepage says "<10h".
- INFERRED: Competitors attack Doppel on price and on the breadth of its brand protection coverage (BrandShield). Doppel attacks the incumbent, legacy vendors (KnowBe4 and "legacy email security").
