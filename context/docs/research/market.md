## United States

### FTC Impersonation Rule and enforcement
- The rule bans impersonating government agencies and businesses in interstate commerce, and lets the FTC go to federal court to get money returned to victims. It was finalized in 2024. Source: https://www.ftc.gov/node/333221 [3P-search]
- On 2026-10-29 the FTC published "FTC Highlights Efforts to Combat Imposter Scams and Protect Consumers". Source: https://www.ftc.gov/node/338570 [P]. Key points:
  - The FTC removed 19 websites impersonating the FTC by sending letters to domain registrars.
  - Enforcement of the Impersonation Rule has produced "more than $80 million in consumer redress" since 2024, across 14 completed actions.
  - Civil penalties go up to $53,088 per violation.
  - In September 2026 the FTC asked for public comment on whether to update the Impersonation Rule, or write a new rule, covering the role of platforms, including ad-optimization tools used in impersonation ads. The Federal Register notice is dated 2026-10-01.
- INFERRED: If platforms become liable for impersonation ads, ad networks and social platforms may respond faster to takedown requests. That would help takedown vendors.

### FTC 2025 fraud data
- Imposter scams in 2025: more than 1 million reports, with losses "increasing by nearly 20% to $3.5 billion". Imposter scams were the top scam type "for the ninth year in a row". "Reports of government imposter scams were up 40%". Source: https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2026/05/new-trends-reports-imposter-scams (2026-05-07) [P]
- All fraud in 2025: about 3 million reports and $15.9B in losses, up from 2.6 million reports and $12B in 2024. Investment scams caused $7.9B of the losses. This figure came from FTC testimony to the Joint Economic Committee on 2026-03-25. Source: https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/ftc-testimony-jec-hearing-on-the-rising-scam-economy.pdf [3P-search; the PDF was not opened]
- The FCC warned about bank impersonation scams in July 2026. Source: https://bankingjournal.aba.com/2026/07/fcc-issues-warning-about-bank-impersonation-scams/ [3P-search]

## United Kingdom: APP fraud reimbursement

- The mandatory reimbursement rule for Authorised Push Payment (APP) fraud started on 2024-10-07. In the first nine months, £112M was reimbursed and 88% of money claimed was returned, up from 66% in 2023/24. Claims fell to 126,000, about 15% lower than the year before. Source: https://www.psr.org.uk/news-and-updates/latest-news/news/one-year-on-impact-of-app-reimbursement-on-victims/ (2025-10-08) [P]
- The independent evaluation by Frontier Economics was published around 2026-06-26 and 2026-07-01. It estimates in-scope losses fell about 21% (around £73M a year). The PSR survey found impersonation fraud increased. A formal consultation is planned for December 2026, and the regime moves to the FCA. Sources: https://www.frontier-economics.com/media/2ctd3jq2/app-fraud-evaluation-final-summary-report-26-06-26.pdf and https://www.regulationtomorrow.com/2026/07/payment-fraud-falls-by-73m-following-psr-reimbursement-scheme/ [3P-search]
- INFERRED: UK banks and payment firms now pay for fraud losses directly. That gives them a cost reason to take down impersonation of their brand before customers are scammed. Doppel lists London as an office (company.md).

## European Union: PSD3 and the Payment Services Regulation (PSR)

- Commentators say the deal moves liability for "spoofing" fraud (someone posing as the customer's own bank) onto payment providers, who must refund the full amount. Online platforms may become liable to those providers if they are told about fraudulent content and fail to remove it. Sources: https://www.biocatch.com/blog/psd3-at-year-end-the-deal-is-done and https://blog.forumforthefuture.be/en/article/payment-services-deal-more-protection-from-online-fraud-and-hidden-fees/29256 [3P-search]
- Cloned websites are reported to be in scope of the draft rules. Source: https://www.fintechfutures.com/paytech/cloned-websites-are-in-the-eus-draft-payment-rules-and-hardly-anyone-has-noticed [3P-search; the page returned 403]
- Timing is unclear. Reports say a provisional agreement was reached in November 2025, with final texts in April 2026 and ECON committee approval in May 2026. The application date is **Not found.**
- INFERRED: As with the UK, banks that refund victims have a reason to remove fake sites quickly, and the "notified and failed to remove" clause gives a takedown request more legal weight.

## Australia: Scams Prevention Framework (SPF)

- The Act came into force on 2025-02-21 as Part IVF of the Competition and Consumer Act. Sources: https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/de-de/wissen/publications/24f9d0e3/finale-world-first-scam-prevention-legislation-has-now-commenced-in-australia and https://www.gtlaw.com.au/insights/the-scams-prevention-framework-legislation-passes-parliament-time-to-get-your-house-in-order [3P-search]
- Banks, telcos and digital platforms were designated as the first sectors, effective 2026-05-23. Draft sector codes for banks and digital platforms were out for consultation until 2026-06-25. Source: https://www.hsfkramer.com/ja/insights/2026-06/stage-1-of-the-scams-prevention-framework [3P-search; the page returned 403]
- Regulators: the ACCC (general), ASIC (banks) and ACMA (telcos). Source: https://www.gadens.com/insights/introduction-of-the-scams-prevention-framework [3P-search]
- One vendor's timeline says AFCA becomes the dispute scheme on 2026-07-01, businesses must join AFCA by 2026-09-01, and consumers can file SPF complaints from 2027-03-31. Source: https://cybelangel.com/blog/social-media-impersonation-regulation-2026/ [3P-search; not confirmed with a government source]
- INFERRED: Under the SPF, regulated firms must "detect, disrupt" and report scams. Doppel's REA Group case study is Australian, so the firm already has a local reference customer.

## Threat trends (urgency)

- Gartner's 2026 CISO survey (n=297): 41% reported a deepfake social engineering incident on an employee audio call in the past 12 months, and 35% to 36% on a video call. Source: https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/09/22/cisos-deepfake-incidents-social-engineering-survey/ [3P-search; the page returned an empty body]
- Mandiant M-Trends 2026 ranks vishing as the second most common initial access vector in 2025, at 11% of intrusions. Source: https://keepnetlabs.com/blog/rise-of-voice-phishing-a-deep-dive-into-vishing-statistics-in-2023 [3P-search, vendor blog citing Mandiant; verify with Mandiant directly]
- CrowdStrike reported a 442% increase in vishing between H1 and H2 2024. Same source [3P-search, verify with CrowdStrike directly]
- Doppel's own framing, from REA Group's CISO: "[Attackers] can scale much more readily with AI tooling." Source: https://www.doppel.com/customer-love [P]
- Many of the vendor-blog statistics found disagree with each other or trace back to no primary source. Only the figures above are used here.
