## Recurring words and ideas
Counted by hand from the quotes above.
- "Agentic" or "AI-native": in both H1s, two release titles and the email product.
- "Social engineering defense" or "SED": the category term, used everywhere.
- "Campaigns, not just messages" or "across every medium": attacks treated as connected campaigns across channels. "Threat Graph" is the named technology ("Doppel 360° Threat Graph").
- "Legacy" or "Traditional" or "yesterday's": how Doppel describes incumbents.
- "Machine speed" and "scale": the framing of the threat.
- "Disrupt", "dismantle", "fight back", "takedown": active, offensive verbs rather than "monitor" or "alert".

## Tone observations (INFERRED)
- INFERRED: The tone is confident and category-defining ("world's first", "leading", "Frontier AI"), but plain in its sentences. It uses short declaratives and contrasts ("Modern threats operate at machine speed. Traditional defenses don't.").
- INFERRED: Some copy is playful and irreverent. The KnowBe4 "farewell" page and the Notion quote ("I'm just a chill guy") show this, along with sports partnerships framed as defense ("Defend the Bay").
- INFERRED: Doppel positions itself on offense versus defense: it takes down attacker infrastructure instead of only alerting.
- INFERRED: Proof is mostly speed and volume (takedown times, takedown counts, POC versus the incumbent), not money saved or ROI. No ROI or cost-saving claims were confirmed on the pages read.
