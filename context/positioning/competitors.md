## Where Doppel wins

| Situation | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Head-to-head trial against an incumbent | REA's two-week POC found more fake apps than the previous year. Zane Lackey: "In just a two-week proof-of-concept, Doppel took down about half of what our incumbent solution did in an entire year." (`customers.md`) |
| The incumbent stopped improving or missed threats | "after two or three years, it was basically the same platform." (unnamed financial services firm). LA firm's takedown rate went from 37% to 76% (`customers.md`) |
| Coverage across many channels | Covers "web, social, messaging, ads, app stores, email, and dark web signals" (`company.md`). It found Facebook and Google ads using a firm's branding (`customers.md`) |
| One vendor for DRP, HRM and email | No researched competitor spans all three (INFERRED from `competitors.md`) |
| Service | "What stood out to me the most about Doppel has been the partnership we've developed," (Advance). "On top of the technology issue was service." (LA firm) (`customers.md`) |

## Where Doppel loses

| Situation | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Price-sensitive buyers | BrandShield claims Doppel is "a premium price point, with higher minimums". Pricing is custom (`competitors.md`, `company.md`) |
| Counterfeit or marketplace-led needs | Red Points and BrandShield lead with counterfeits; Doppel's proof is impersonation and phishing (INFERRED from `competitors.md`, `customers.md`) |
| "Fastest takedown" comparisons | Allure claims "~15 min" to block, Netcraft "33 min." (unconfirmed). Doppel's homepage says "<10h" (`competitors.md`, `company.md`) |
| Scale in training | KnowBe4 claims "70K+ organizations", Adaptive "2,000+"; Doppel has 300+ customers across all products (`competitors.md`, `company.md`) |
| Analyst-led buyers | ZeroFox is a Gartner MQ Leader (CTI). Doppel's Gartner Market Guide status: Not found (`competitors.md`) |

## How to respond

Objections are paraphrased, not quotes. BrandShield's exact wording is in `competitors.md`.

| Objection or rival claim (paraphrased) | Response | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Too expensive | Compare on results in a two-week POC, not on list price | REA and a16z POC results (`customers.md`) |
| A rival's takedowns are faster | Ask what the median covers. Doppel publishes "<10h" across domains, social and paid ads, and "under one hour" for phishing domains | `company.md` |
| Doppel is strongest in phishing, not brand protection (BrandShield: "Doppel's detection strength lies primarily in phishing.") | Point to brand protection results across social, domains and apps | Coinbase, Sugar23, Dapper Labs (`customers.md`) |
| Enforcement needs a lot of customer input (BrandShield: "Enforcement scope is narrow and requires input from the customer.") | Onboarding takes minutes | Advance: "It was a single email." (`customers.md`) |
| We already have KnowBe4 | Doppel adds attack simulation across channels, alongside KnowBe4 or replacing it | "a full farewell or adding a plus-one alongside your current platform" (`signals.md`) |
