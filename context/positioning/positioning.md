## Positioning statement

For security teams at organizations whose brand and executives are being impersonated across domains, social media, ads, apps and email, Doppel is the Social Engineering Defense platform. It unifies Digital Risk Protection, Human Risk Management and Email Security, finds attacks as connected campaigns, and takes down the attacker's assets at the registrar, social platform, ad network and telco. Unlike an incumbent brand protection vendor or manual reporting, it has shown more takedowns in a two-week proof of concept than incumbents managed in a year (REA, a16z), and cut Advance's domain takedowns from 30 days to under a week.
